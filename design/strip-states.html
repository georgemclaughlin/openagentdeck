Question grid · the documented maximum

Four options, multiSelect, question 2 of 3 — six targets, six cells

The worst case the schema allows: 4 options + Other + DONE = six targets in the fixed 3×2 grid of 90 px rows (10.3 mm — a declared exception to the 110 px single-row floor, still far above the 64 px minimum). Staging is selected (amber ring + ✓), so DONE is armed; with nothing selected DONE renders disabled as hairline + dim ink — never faint. The stepper reads 2 OF 3 in the well head; advancing re-arms targets at 400 ms, the 120 s budget spans all three questions, and answers ship together after the last. Demoting this takeover keeps the hold; re-promotion restarts at question 1.

State vocabulary · color is never alone
